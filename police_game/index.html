POLICE & ROBBER

— PROLOGUE —

The city of Nova Heights was once peaceful...

But everything changed when the Crimson Crew — a ruthless gang of robbers — broke out of Blackgate Prison. They spread across the city, looting banks, robbing stores, and terrorizing innocent people.

That's you. You are Officer Atif, the youngest cop on the force. Your mission: arrest robbers before time runs out.

NEW: The GREEN CIRCLE around your vehicle is your arrest range. Upgrade the BIGGER CIRCLE in the shop to make the green circle larger and catch robbers from further away!

Earn coins from every mission. Buy gadgets like the Grapple Hook, Portal Gun, and Time Freeze.

Good luck, Officer.
